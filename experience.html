Execution Framework

How I Deliver Business Value

The repeatable 5-step methodology I use to take software from raw concept to verified production release.

Phase 01

Discovery & Stakeholder Elicitation

Interviewing key business owners to uncover underlying pain points, user personas, operational constraints, and quantitative success metrics.

Phase 02

BRD & User Story Engineering

Structuring unambiguous functional specs, data models, edge case rules, and acceptance criteria using Gherkin-style scenarios.

Phase 03

Wireframing & Flow Validation

Translating written requirements into interactive wireframe blueprints and user flowcharts for client pre-development signoff.

Phase 04

Sprint Handoff & Tech Guidance

Serving as the direct bridge to engineering teams, clarifying architectural questions, and evaluating Change Requests (CRs).

Phase 05

UAT & Production Signoff

Conducting User Acceptance Testing against original acceptance criteria, documenting gaps, and securing client milestone approvals.
