About Suraj

The Mind Behind the Systems

A deep look into my background, academic qualifications, core disciplines, and how I approach bridging business strategy with technical execution.

I am an M.Sc. Information Technology professional currently serving as a Business Analyst at MSquare Software Systems Pvt. Ltd. in Jalna, Maharashtra.

My day-to-day work centers around eliciting, structuring, and defending requirements for mission-critical client products — spanning an interactive chess learning academy, a comprehensive apartment society management suite, a pet marketplace, and a dedicated finance recruitment portal.

Unlike traditional business analysts who stop at documentation, I bring a solid, hands-on engineering foundation. Having developed full-stack web platforms using Angular, mobile applications using Ionic Framework, and backend utilities with Python, I understand how software is actually architected, tested, and deployed.

"Great software fails not from code complexity, but from ambiguous requirements. By mastering both requirement engineering and code execution, I ensure ideas translate cleanly into high-performing production systems."

I actively incorporate AI-assisted workflows and MCP server testing into my workflow to prototype user journeys rapidly, validate technical feasibility, and shorten the feedback loop between client signoff and sprint kickoff.
